Note on model fit. The published specification leaves the residual
covariance between the alexithymia mediator and the somatic mediator
fixed at zero (and, in the parallel model, the three subscale-somatic
residual covariances), so the models are over-identified rather than
saturated and the chi-square is significant. This is the specification
used in 12_sem_pathway_analysis.R and 17_revision_sensitivity.R and is
reproduced here unchanged. Both models are recursive with uncorrelated
disturbances, so every structural equation is estimated equation-by-
equation and its ML coefficients equal the OLS coefficients; this is
verified numerically above, where the OLS simple slopes in Part 1a match
the SEM defined parameters in Part 1b to four decimal places.
