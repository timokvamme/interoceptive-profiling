#############################################################################
SUMMARY: KEY FINDINGS
#############################################################################

1. MODERATION:
   - IAS×IATS interaction significantly predicts somatic outcomes
   - IAS×IATS does NOT significantly predict TAS (alexithymia)
   - Pattern is consistent for full and minimal scales

2. DUAL-PATHWAY SEM:
   - TAS pathway: IAS → TAS → MH (additive effects, no interaction)
   - Somatic pathway: IAS×IATS → Somatic → MH (moderated mediation)
   - The interaction specifically affects the somatic pathway

3. SENSITIVITY ANALYSES:
   - Results hold for MH composite and individual scales (PHQ, GAD, STAI)
   - Results hold for SSS-8 alone and SSS-8+PCS combined
   - Results hold for full (42) and minimal (21) item scales

4. CLINICAL IMPLICATION:
   - High IAS with high IATS (Hypervigilant profile) shows protective
     effect on somatic symptoms that reduces mental health burden
   - Low IAS with any IATS (Uncertain profile) shows elevated somatic
     symptoms driving worse mental health
