#############################################################################
SUMMARY
#############################################################################

1. VVIQ correlates with IAS (r = +.21) but not IATS (r = -.04, n.s.),
   confirming specificity to interoceptive accuracy.

2. IAS mediates 28.6% of the VVIQ -> TAS association
   (indirect = -0.0697, 95% CI [-0.0994, -0.0445]).

3. Mediation is specific to IAS (indirect = -0.0655, p = 0.0000)
   not IATS (indirect = -0.0121, p = 0.2585).

4. Subscale decomposition: VVIQ -> IAS targets primarily
   DIF (37.4%), then DDF (34.8%), EOT negligible (27.8%).

5. Imagery group x cluster (chi-square): chi-sq(6) = 27.48, p = 0.0001,
   Cramer's V = 0.129, N = 832.
   Imagery groups are NOT evenly distributed across cluster profiles -
   inspect Part 5 standardized residuals for which cells drive the effect.

6. THEORETICAL IMPLICATION:
   The imagery-alexithymia link is substantially mediated by
   interoceptive accuracy, supporting the shared-mechanism
   hypothesis (IMAGERY <-- INTEROCEPTION --> EMOTION) over the
   amplification view (IMAGERY --> EMOTION). Imagery vividness
   and emotional identification share a common dependence on
   accurate internal signal detection.
