FIGURE 2. Interoceptive profile clustering and outcomes.
========================================================

Panel A: Correlation heatmap (lower triangle) for IATS, IAS, TAS, SSS-8, PCS,
PHQ-9, GAD-7, and STAI. Cell colors encode sign and significance; values are
Pearson r. This panel provides the variable-level context used for clustering
and outcome contrasts.

Panel B: Scatter of IAS vs IATS with 90% ellipses for the 3 k-means profiles
(Efficient, Hypervigilant, Uncertain). Colors match profile labels across the
figure.

Panels C-H: Outcome distributions by profile, shown as box plots with Tukey HSD
markers. Boxes show the median and interquartile range, whiskers extend to 1.5
times the interquartile range, grey points show values beyond the whiskers, and
the white diamond marks the group mean. TAS (C), SSS-8 (D), PCS (E), PHQ-9 (F),
GAD-7 (G), and STAI (H). These panels show how the profiles differ across
alexithymia, somatic symptoms, pain, depression, anxiety, and trait anxiety.

Sample: N = 832.
